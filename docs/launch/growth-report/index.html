Method

What the numbers mean

The public launch boundary is July 7 UTC. The first stable PyPI upload on July 2 was a prerelease period for this analysis, not the launch itself.

“Users” is a proxy

Package indexes publish downloads, not unique people. CI, reinstalls, upgrades, and multiple machines can create multiple downloads per person.

Mirrors are removed

The main series excludes mirror traffic. Including mirrors raises the all-time observed count to 14,912, but that is less useful as a human-adoption signal.

The launch date is behavioral

Traffic rose from 62 views on July 6 to 295 on July 7 and 973 on July 8. The concentrated star wave began late on July 7 UTC.
